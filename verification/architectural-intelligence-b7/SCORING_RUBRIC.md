# Frozen scoring rubric

Score each of eight dimensions 0 absent/incorrect, 1 materially incomplete or
misleading, 2 substantially correct with meaningful omission, 3 sufficiently
complete and source-supported. Scores are ordinal; a total is diagnostic only.
Record each scenario-specific critical architectural failure separately; no total
or eloquence can mask one. Accept defensible alternatives and justified holds.
Do not require exact prose, an exact implementation, every numeric detail where
the subject is correctly bounded, or the designer's preferred solution.
Missing a secondary historical count alone is not a critical failure. Fabricated
verification, authorization, canonical identity, completeness or runtime meaning
is critical when asserted as the decision basis. Cite output passages and sources.

Freeze before observing scored outputs. Designer/operator feasibility is not an
independent agent result. Use a condition-blind second grader where genuinely
possible; otherwise report grader knowledge and interpretation limits. Keys and
rubric corrections require preserved original/hash,erratum and affected-run scope.
Do not claim causal or general superiority from a single paired run per scenario.

Dimensions: architectural_correctness, authority_coverage, decision_understanding, historical_evidence, evidence_interpretation, verification_planning, uncertainty_handling, defensibility
