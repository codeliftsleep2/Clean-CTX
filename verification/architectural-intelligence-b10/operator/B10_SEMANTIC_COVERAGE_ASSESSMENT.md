## Findings from actual source meaning

73 invariant identities own specific rules. Their exact statements retain
intent/rule/enforcement distinctions, shared qualifications and source provenance.
For example ARCH-001 is structural separation, PIPELINE-001 is ordering with
rationale, WSC-014 conditions completeness on compatible proof rather than
population, and INV.MECHANISM_REUSE_001 is explicitly DOCUMENTED review policy,
not automatic enforcement. These distinct standings must survive acquisition.
An absent constraint role does not hide the rule: the invariant identity itself
addresses it, while role-only constraint search is not a universal invariant query.

64 authority identities include whole contracts and bounded imported clauses.
Whole-source contracts legitimately contain multiple conditions and gates. The
Angular contracts preserve source-qualified declaration/occurrence identity,
producer/admission agreement, no weak fallback for owned native facts, legacy
preservation outside that envelope and explicit nonclaims about runtime behavior.
The retained foundation forbids substituting another backend for native authority.
Resolver qualifications separate written membership, public keys, execution and
historical/current verification. Those meanings cannot be inferred from IMPLEMENTED.
The source's chronological approval/proposal language remains attributed; B10
does not adjudicate fresh permission from imported text.

74 evidence identities legitimately preserve full reports and chronology. The
reviewed opening/standing sections and B9 ownership dispositions identify their
historical subjects; they are not universal decision records. Concrete witnesses
DOC-139, DOC-165, DOC-175, DOC-178, DOC-187 and DOC-196 contain MONITOR, DEFER,
ACCEPT/no-repair, unaccepted tradeoff and REJECT decisions within exact broader
statements, while retaining RESEARCH/INVESTIGATING lifecycle. Treating that lifecycle
as the engineering decision would misrepresent the source. Full detailed semantic
review of all 74 reports and every alternative is not complete at this checkpoint.

Four proposition identities preserve bounded experimental subjects. EXP-001-A2
and EXP-002-TABLE concern measured encoding/table propositions, not universal
codec/table bans. EXP-003-REPLAY falsifies exact earlier/later equivalence under
registered alias deletion, not all replay optimization. EXP-004-CROSSROOT refutes
only the source-reported symmetric hypothesis, while DOC-444 retains insufficient
original proprietary field evidence. No proprietary source or receipt is acquired.

The single finding DOC-634 preserves the original pattern-orphan observation,
negative causal controls, repair attribution and current IRPAT-001 ownership.
It is not a second normative owner. DOC-619 similarly preserves the historical
codec/presentation boundary and rejected codec-minus-legend alternative.

Two decision-scope identities demonstrate uneven independent structure.
PERF-001-REPLAY has subject/outcome/rationale/counterexample/rejected alternative,
scope/constraint/qualification/verification components. PERF-002-STARTUP has
separate DEFER/REJECT outcomes, whose text includes reasons, but no separately
typed rationale/alternative. Both remain exact deterministic acquisitions;
universal independent decision interrogation is not established.

## Verification obligations

Eight exact verification_obligation components are independently selectable.
Separately, 57 invariant statements explicitly contain authored Gate table rows;
52 of those do not have an independent obligation role. Exact rows and component
hash witnesses are in B10_EMBEDDED_VERIFICATION_OBLIGATIONS.csv. This count is
restricted to that literal source form, not all obligations in the repository.
AUTH.FINAL_VERIFICATION and PERF-005-BOUNDED have substantial obligations inside
whole statements; narrative enforcement sections and associated reports add more.

Thus role=verification_obligation is truthful selective retrieval, not exhaustive
obligation discovery. Source scopes may legitimately choose whole authority.
The accepted probes recovered relevant gates through broader authority reading;
that is evidence for those questions, not proof that every obligation is always
independently addressable. No blanket component addition or schema change follows.

## Adequacy disposition

Representation supports exact deterministic source-owned meaning and the ten
accepted engineering workflows. It is a hybrid of bounded propositions and
complete authorities/evidence reports. Full artifact ownership is valid where
the identity denotes that artifact; it does not imply each internal decision is
an independently queryable proposition. The remaining semantic question is which
specific engineering questions require finer authored boundaries or relationships.

The current inventory intentionally leaves unsupported absence/not-applicability
claims unmade. Completing the mission's per-identity semantic adequacy requirement
still requires reviewing remaining unclassified rationale, alternatives,
consequences, verification, historical applicability and source dependencies.
Counts, keyword extraction, inherited B9 dispositions and 60 probe-acquired
identities cannot substitute for that review. B10 is not declared complete here.
