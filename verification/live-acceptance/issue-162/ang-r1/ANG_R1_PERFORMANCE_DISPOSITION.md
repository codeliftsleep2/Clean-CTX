## Applicable rule

typed dependency/preservation contexts. AF-PERF-002 requires demonstrated problem,

## Method applicability and decision

The direct P0 fixture is still present and core implementation unchanged, so its
work-count methodology remains meaningful. It cannot measure current startup,
transport/provider latency, full capture cost, SQLite cost or public key output
that does not exist yet. The original broad scripts include source formatting and
wide historical selectors; do not blindly rerun them as current acceptance.

No currently failing performance gate or measured key-registration scaling defect
was established in ANG-R1. Remaining optimization opportunities are **not** all
mandatory resumption prerequisites. Historical performance-first suspension was
an explicit owner sequencing decision; this investigation cannot silently revoke
it. The owner can now authorize a bounded public-key design without implicitly
approving witness reuse, lock changes or general optimization. New production
key work still needs its own exact public contract and applicable AF-PERF-001
tracked coverage/current verification. A failed relevant gate would then block
acceptance of that change.

Do not introduce token savings as an Angular criterion. Correct relationship
membership, identity, provenance, refusal, diagnostics, currentness, history and
lifecycle remain mandatory even if an alternative is faster.
