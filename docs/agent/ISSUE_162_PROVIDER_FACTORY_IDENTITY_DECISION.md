## Alternatives and approval boundary

Recommended: extend the existing provider-pair mechanism with explicit factory
field/function-declaration policy. It preserves exact identities and source
ownership while adding bounded admission and lifecycle verification work.

Rejected: name equality, treating a factory as its returned service, accepting
all callable-looking expressions, or globally weakening relation admission for
external subjects. These lose identity, invent runtime meaning or affect
unrelated relation contracts.

Deferral retains the lower-bound factory projection and leaves this demonstrated
identity question open. Generation-56 class/registration evidence remains valid
for its narrower scope and cannot certify factory authority.

Shared semantic identity, relation admission, durable meaning and index identity
are affected. AGENTS.md's Architectural Approval Gate requires explicit owner
approval before implementation. No production repair has been made.
