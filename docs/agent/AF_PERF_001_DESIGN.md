## Separate authority-validation and lock design boundary

Its design must bind exact immutable authority/source hashes and typed witness expectations; specify entry/exit currentness observations, concurrent external filesystem edits, contradictory observations, dependency/config/package identity, publication/revision rechecks and ownership.
