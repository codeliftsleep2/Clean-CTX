## Scope excluded: requires broader authority-validation design

Repeated full witness validation inside mapping/arrow/native role proof, repeated
route-root/facet/demanded-occurrence scans, and snapshot currentness/admission under
semantic_edge_snapshots mutex remain separate risks. One projection per relation
will not prove these are bounded or fast. #173 NgRx global refinement and #174 core
index semantic-density complexity remain independent outstanding paths.

A future shared operation-scoped validation mechanism must define:

- Exact authority/source hash, dependency/config/package and typed witness identity.
  Conflicting expectations must refuse rather than collapse solely by path.
- Currentness observation at operation entry and required consumption/publication
  boundaries. A request with intervening hydration/publication is not one stable
  operation; never grant cross-call or stale-cache authority.
- Concurrent filesystem-edit behavior: what is reobserved, when a changed witness
  invalidates the operation, and truthful failure/retry behavior. No atomic external
  filesystem snapshot may be claimed merely from one initial check.
- Publication/revision checks tying the validated authority to the exact snapshot/
  index generation consumed or published. A candidate immutable clone alone does
  not prove the live owner/revision still matches at commit/exposure.
- Lock ownership and race protocol before moving IO outside a mutex: identify who
  pins the snapshot/revision, who validates, who publishes, and how races refuse.
  Retry limits or broader lifecycle policy require their own explicit disposition.

No shared context, witness receipt, TTL/cache, snapshot-lock or revision protocol
is implemented by this proposal or by resolver P0.
