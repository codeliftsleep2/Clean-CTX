## Proposed systemic defect closure standard

A closure record must establish all of: confirmed root cause and evidence class;
controlling invariant/source revision; applicable producer/consumer/lifecycle map;
approved preserving repair; historical exact RED or explicit missing-proof limit;
post-repair exact GREEN; applicable AFF property; mandatory exact GARC execution;
actual graph admission and source provenance; explicit remaining exposure and
field applicability. The original regression alone cannot establish systemic closure.

Where automatic proof is incomplete, document the strongest check, unresolved
cases, owner disposition and reopening conditions. A completed local test cannot
bank later production edits or certify another consumer/feature. Initial actual
root-cause/repair evidence must remain discoverable through governed source links.
