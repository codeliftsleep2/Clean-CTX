## Census method and bounds

Direct call searches followed compile_candidates_for, semantic_compiler().compile,
compile_source_artifact, source_inventory, program_units, resolver/bootstrap
preparation, and currentness/retry consumers. Relevant source bodies and guards
were read. This is not a complete whole-program call graph or an allocation proof.
The November 19 audit records exact file/function references for A01–A13.

Shared repairs belong at the mechanism owning equivalent authority and lifetime.
Discovery predicates and failure semantics remain specialized where contracts differ.
No generic registry/cache or global retry/concurrency rule is authorized by exposure.
