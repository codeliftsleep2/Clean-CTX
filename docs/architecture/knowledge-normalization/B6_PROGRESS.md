## Mission and priority

B6 includes Mission Addendum 01. Correctness, completeness of applicable
architectural knowledge, decision reasoning, evidence integrity and practical
discoverability precede retrieval efficiency. Admission is based on architectural
value, exact subject and provenance, not token cost. Larger relevant historical
sources are retained where they explain decisions or verification boundaries.
Growth measurements are diagnostics, not a savings target or acceptance threshold.

The post-B6 handoff is the [Architectural Knowledge Completeness and Decision
Intelligence Assessment](ARCHITECTURAL_KNOWLEDGE_COMPLETENESS_AND_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_ASSESSMENT.md).
It replaces the proposed token-centric roadmap. B6 does not implement retrieval
remediation or predetermine that future remediation must reduce context size.
