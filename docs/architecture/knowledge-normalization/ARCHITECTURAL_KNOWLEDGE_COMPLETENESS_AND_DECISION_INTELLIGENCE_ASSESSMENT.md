## Diagnostic dimensions, not presumed causes

For an identified availability/usability problem, measure unique and repeated
source bytes, original historical artifact volume, evidence associations,
relationship/witness representation, JSON escaping/serialization overhead and
legitimate transitive closure. Source and artifact sharing are distinct from
semantic identity, currentness and actual proof. A successful lookup or warm cache
does not establish acquisition completeness or present truth.

The catalog and source inventories can identify candidate contributors, but none
of these dimensions is established as the cause of an overflow without measurement.
Do not bypass production finite limits to manufacture a claimed complete response.
Do not compare unavailable complete payloads or unrelated B2–B6 workloads as if
they were controlled longitudinal performance measurements.
